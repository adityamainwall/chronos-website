CHRONOS is a personal digital life analytics application designed to track, preserve and analyse your Android screen-time history over extended periods.
CHRONOS maintains its own historical usage database and provides long-term analytics, application-wise history, calendar views and cloud synchronisation. It is designed to preserve collected records independently of the temporary history available in Digital Wellbeing.
