
# CHRONOS
### Your Time. Your Digital History.

CHRONOS is a project focused on helping people understand, track and preserve their digital activity over time.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://chronos.adityamainwal.workers.dev/

## About

We spend hours on our devices every day. But where does all that time go?

CHRONOS aims to turn everyday device usage into meaningful long-term information, helping users explore their habits, discover patterns and understand where their time goes.

## Website Features

- Modern, responsive landing page
- Overview of CHRONOS features
- Daily time tracking introduction
- Calendar history preview
- Digital activity analytics preview
- App download section
- FAQ and project information
